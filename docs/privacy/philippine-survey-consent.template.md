# Do not field as written

This is a requirements template, not approved legal copy or a translation. The
named data controller, provider, Data Protection Officer, Philippine counsel,
research owner, and human Filipino-language reviewer must approve each deployed
version and preserve its checksum.

# Required notice fields

- Study/controller: `[legal entity, address, DPO contact, study ID]`
- Survey provider/processor: `[provider, contact, subcontractors]`
- Sponsor/payer: `[name and contact]`
- Purpose: show one randomly assigned political/campaign message and measure
  aggregate comprehension, reaction, trust, relevance, persuasiveness,
  consideration, and optional share intent for SIMULA research/calibration.
- Data collected: opaque project response ID, assignment, broad region cohort,
  survey answers, consent/completion/quality flags, and approved weighting
  variables. List every actual field.
- Data not collected by SIMULA: name, contact information, voter ID, precise
  address, reusable panel ID, or individual targeting/persuadability score.
- Processing: quality screening, weighting, aggregate statistical comparison,
  model training/testing only if expressly approved, and derived aggregate
  reports. Explain material automated processing in plain language.
- Recipients/locations: `[SIMULA entity, provider, approved subprocessors,
  hosting countries, transfer safeguard]`.
- Retention/deletion: `[raw provider and SIMULA periods, aggregate retention,
  deletion or anonymization event, audit evidence]`.
- Voluntary choice: participation is voluntary; refusing or withdrawing before
  the stated cutoff has no penalty. Explain provider incentive consequences.
- Rights: access, correction, objection, erasure/blocking, complaint, withdrawal,
  and contact/escalation route, subject to lawful research/verification limits.
- Risks/limits: political questions may be sensitive; state safeguards and any
  residual risk. Do not promise anonymity unless the assessment supports it.
- Publication: describe aggregate publication, minimum-cell suppression, and
  applicable election-survey disclosures/controlled verification access.

# Consent action

Use an unticked affirmative action after the full notice:

`[ ] I have read and understood the notice. I voluntarily consent to the stated
survey, political-message exposure, data transfer, aggregate analysis, approved
model use, retention, and publication/verification purposes.`

Record notice version, language, timestamp, withdrawal state, and evidence of the
action. Separate any optional recontact, marketing, unrelated research, or new
model purpose into granular consent. Public availability or panel membership is
not consent for this study.
