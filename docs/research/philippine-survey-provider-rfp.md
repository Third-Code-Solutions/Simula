# Request

SIMULA requests a proposal for a Philippine randomized split-ballot campaign-
message study. Please quote a probability-sample national study and identify any
faster nonprobability pilot separately. Do not combine their claims or pricing.

# Population and design

- Target universe: quote Philippine adults aged 18+ and registered Philippine
  voters separately.
- Coverage: all 17 regions, with proposed region/age/sex/urbanicity allocation,
  frame source, selection probabilities, and expected incidence.
- Experiment: server-side random assignment to control and message variants;
  one exposure per respondent; assignment and deviation logs delivered.
- Sample size: provider statistician to supply inputs for a prospective power
  analysis, including design effect, clustering, attrition, cell count, and
  smallest detectable effect. SIMULA will approve the analysis before launch.
- Languages/mode: state supported interview modes and human translation,
  back-translation, cognitive testing, interviewer, and device controls.
- Holdout: quote an untouched respondent holdout and a later independent wave.

# Required deliverables

1. frozen questionnaire, stimulus archive, translations, programming test link,
   randomization specification, and instrument checksums;
2. sampling/recruitment plan, field dates, PSU/stratum information where lawful,
   disposition and response metrics, quota deviations, base/final weights,
   design effect, effective sample size, and weighting report;
3. deidentified respondent rows using the SIMULA data dictionary, with opaque
   project-only response IDs, assignment, region cohort, outcomes, quality,
   consent, completion, bot/fraud, and weighting fields;
4. codebook, fieldwork report, version/correction log, payload SHA-256, and signed
   delivery manifest;
5. aggregate tables and a controlled verification copy sufficient for applicable
   COMELEC inspection duties without exposing the file publicly;
6. deletion certificate after the agreed retention period.

No name, email, phone, voter ID, precise address, reusable panel ID, contact
record, individual political targeting score, or provider recontact key may be
delivered to SIMULA.

# Contract and privacy requirements

The proposal must identify the Philippine PIC/PIP roles, subcontractors, hosting
countries, transfer mechanism, security controls, breach notice, audit evidence,
retention, deletion, withdrawal, and data-subject-right workflows. Consent must
cover political questions, creative exposure, deidentified transfer, aggregate
calibration, model training/testing where applicable, derived outputs, client-
facing SaaS use, publication, recipients, retention, and cross-border processing.

The executed SOW/license must expressly authorize perpetual commercial internal
calibration/testing, derived coefficients and model artifacts, aggregate SaaS
and client output, publication, verification, and lawful archival evidence. It
must prohibit reidentification, unrelated linkage, person-level targeting, and
vendor reuse outside the consented purpose unless separately authorized.

# Proposal response

Return:

- feasibility and exclusions for each target universe and mode;
- sample and wave options with itemized design, programming, recruitment,
  incentives, translation, fieldwork, data, reporting, taxes, and rush costs;
- realistic schedule from approved instrument to verified delivery;
- quality/fraud controls and expected rejection/replacement policy;
- example deidentified schema, weighting report, fieldwork report, and contract;
- exact respondent-level, commercial/model, publication, SaaS/client, retention,
  and transfer rights offered;
- conflicts, political-work restrictions, sponsor disclosure, and COMELEC support;
- named methodology, privacy, security, and contract contacts.

Selection is conditional on methodology review, DPO/PIA approval, Philippine
counsel review, source-rights approval, and a successful non-production import
rehearsal. A public topline product without deidentified rows and assignment
metadata is not equivalent to this request.
