# SIMULA User Discovery Plan

## Current evidence

- `OBSERVED`: public products offer campaign or research simulation workflows (E-1001, E-1003, E-1008, E-1013).
- `REPORTED`: vendors describe speed, scale, and validation benefits; protocols and independent replication are incomplete.
- `REPORTED`: independent studies show bounded usefulness and material cross-group, variance, drift, and country-performance risks (E-1015, E-1016, E-2003–E-2005).
- `UNKNOWN`: whether target Philippine teams will adopt or pay for SIMULA, which workflow is highest value, and which evidence earns trust.

## Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| H-JOB-01 | Brand/agency strategists need a faster way to identify risky wording before human research/media spend. | Fewer than three of five qualified participants describe a recent comparable decision or existing workaround. |
| H-JOB-02 | Transparent experimental output is more useful than opaque “AI audience” certainty. | Participants consistently prefer certainty claims or cannot act on disclosed limits. |
| H-JOB-03 | A text-only, one-audience pretest is a useful first slice. | Most participants require image/video, custom audiences, or variant comparison before any value exists. |
| H-TRUST-01 | Type labels, provenance, and limitations prevent overinterpretation. | Any participant treats demo output as measured population opinion after the task. |

## Recruitment and method

- Five to eight Philippines-focused brand, agency, insights, or communications practitioners.
- Mix strategists and research leads; exclude project team and AI-product vendors.
- Forty-five-minute semi-structured interview plus prototype task.
- Record sponsor, participant criteria, dates, language, instrument version, consent, analysis method, exclusions, and limitations per E-4011.
- Do not collect client-confidential stimuli; use the authored demo scenario.
- Store notes de-identified and access-controlled; set deletion date before collection.

## Instrument

1. Describe the last time wording or campaign risk was discovered too late.
2. What decision was made, with what evidence, delay, and cost?
3. Which current workaround was used? What failed?
4. Ask participant to create a project, run the demo, and interpret the result without coaching.
5. Ask what the output means, does not mean, and what they would do next.
6. Ask them to locate provenance, limitations, and generated-content labels.
7. Ask which missing capability prevents use and which claim would reduce trust.

## Decision rule

- Continue toward staging only if H-JOB-01 and H-TRUST-01 pass.
- Narrow or reposition if H-JOB-03 fails but a smaller validated job emerges.
- Stop customer-facing release if users reliably overinterpret results despite disclosure.
- Negative evidence is retained. No retroactive threshold changes.
