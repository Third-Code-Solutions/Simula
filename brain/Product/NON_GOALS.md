# SIMULA Non-Goals

- Replacing surveys, panels, interviews, focus groups, field experiments, or expert judgment.
- Guaranteeing campaign, conversion, election, policy, or behavior outcomes.
- Claiming representation of 70 million Filipinos or any population without independently validated evidence.
- Presenting generated personas as real individuals or generated rationales as participant quotations.
- Generating one LLM request per supposed citizen.
- Inventing demographic, behavioral, or psychographic distributions.
- Using fictional psychological labels without validated constructs.
- Copying competitor branding, layouts, copy, assets, animations, or proprietary workflow.
- Reconstructing or claiming knowledge of competitor private data, methods, models, accuracy, code, or infrastructure.
- Payment gateway or subscription billing in current scope.
- Automated high-stakes decisions about individuals or protected groups.
- Scraping public personal profiles to construct audiences without a lawful, rights-reviewed, terms-compliant purpose.
- Optimizing political persuasion, vulnerable-group manipulation, or sensitive-trait inference.
- Production deployment during Phase 0 or without explicit authorization later.
