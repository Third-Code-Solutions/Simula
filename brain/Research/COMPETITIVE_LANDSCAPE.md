# Competitive Landscape

## Question

Which public workflows, proof patterns, and gaps shape synthetic-audience products relevant to Philippine campaign and research teams?

## Public comparison

| Product | Public job/workflow | Public evidence pattern | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Predikta | Pre-launch audience definition, campaign simulation, sentiment/risk/recommendations | Vendor page, associated preprint, involved-partner backtest | Population frame, general accuracy, calibration, and independent replication UNKNOWN |
| Netopia AI | Behavioral-model company and reusable intelligence engine | Vendor description plus same associated research/backtest | Private data, method, architecture, and validation scope UNKNOWN |
| Aaru | Multi-agent population/decision simulation | Vendor and partner claim | Public reproducible protocol absent |
| Evidenza | Synthetic market research and validation comparisons | Vendor aggregate and hosted testimonials | Source datasets and study-level error detail absent |
| Synthetic Users | Fast qualitative discovery with synthetic personas | Vendor parity claim plus explicit real-user caveat | Qualitative parity is not outcome validation |
| SyntheticAudience.ai | Persona interrogation plus claimed live-panel RCT | Vendor thresholds and workflow | Named studies, raw results, and independent audit absent |

Evidence IDs and source limitations: E-1001–E-1016 in [[../EVIDENCE_LEDGER|Evidence Ledger]].

## Common patterns

- REPORTED, high (E-1001, E-1011–E-1014): vendors position short time-to-insight, audience/persona construction, quantitative/qualitative summaries, recommendations, or comparison.
- INFERRED, medium (E-1005, E-1006, E-1011–E-1014): public validation claims often lack enough protocol/data detail for independent reproduction.
- INFERRED, high (E-1002, E-1005–E-1016): hidden population provenance, validation scope, uncertainty, and generated-versus-measured ambiguity create a credible trust risk. Actual buyer demand for these controls is UNKNOWN.
- REPORTED, medium (E-1013): one vendor explicitly retains real-user validation and positions its product as a discovery copilot.

## Independent evidence

- E-1015 REPORTED, high: large, peer-reviewed cognitive-model work demonstrates bounded predictive feasibility but acknowledges population and individual-difference limitations.
- E-1016 REPORTED, high: cross-country LLM work reports larger errors in underrepresented countries and rejects replacing direct human self-report.
- E-2004/E-2005 REPORTED, high: demographic steering can remain misaligned; survey-like means may conceal variance compression, changed inference, prompt sensitivity, and drift.

## SIMULA opportunity hypotheses

- PROPOSED: lead with “pressure-test before field research,” not “replace research.”
- PROPOSED: every headline result shows experimental/validated status, population/data/method/model version, uncertainty/stability, and unsupported slices.
- PROPOSED: optimize for Philippine languages and contexts only where benchmark evidence exists.
- PROPOSED: provide a transparent comparison workflow and separately stored real-world feedback for calibration.
- PROPOSED: never copy competitor brand, language, layouts, assets, or unverifiable coverage claims.

Public pages prove product availability and positioning, not market demand, adoption, revenue, or user outcomes. Those remain UNKNOWN until recorded customer/design-partner evidence exists.

## Phase 1 questions

- Which first user/job has enough urgency and benchmark data to justify an MVP?
- Which report metrics have defensible constructs and human-comparison data?
- Which languages, regions, and categories are explicitly unsupported at launch?
- What evidence promotion ladder changes an output from experimental to calibrated?
