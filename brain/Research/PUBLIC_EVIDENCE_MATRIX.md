## Conclusion

- UNKNOWN: public availability and positioning do not establish market demand, adoption, revenue, or user outcomes.
- REPORTED, medium (E-1001, E-1011–E-1014): vendors consistently position faster pre-launch or research feedback as a benefit.
- INFERRED, high (E-1002, E-1005–E-1016, E-2004, E-2005): provenance, scope-limited validation, uncertainty, and reproducibility are credible differentiation dimensions; product demand for those dimensions remains unvalidated.
- UNKNOWN: competitor private data, models, algorithms, calibration, customer outcomes, and infrastructure.
