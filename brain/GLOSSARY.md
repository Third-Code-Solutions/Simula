# SIMULA Glossary

- **Audience cell:** Aggregated group defined by a defensible combination of cohort attributes and associated weight; not an individual citizen.
- **Calibration:** Adjustment and evaluation process comparing predictions with held-out human or outcome data.
- **Cohort:** Audience subset defined for a study or analysis.
- **Confidence / uncertainty:** Qualified expression of estimate stability or evidential support; not a generic LLM self-rating.
- **Evidence classification:** One of `OBSERVED`, `REPORTED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `PROPOSED`.
- **Ground truth:** Separately stored observed human responses or outcomes used for evaluation; never silently blended with predictions.
- **Heuristic estimate:** Number produced by an explicit rule or unvalidated model; must not be presented as measured fact.
- **Population frame:** Defined target population, coverage, source distributions, exclusions, and weighting basis.
- **Provenance:** Traceable source, license, collection context, transformation, version, validation status, and limitations of data or output.
- **Psychographic construct:** Research-defined latent characteristic measured through a documented instrument; not a fictional persona label.
- **Stimulus:** Message, slogan, headline, proposition, brief, offer, script, or later media asset evaluated in a study.
- **Synthetic audience:** Artificial profiles or weighted cells generated from documented assumptions and data; not real people and not automatically representative.
- **Validation status:** Explicit level of empirical support for a dataset, method, model, metric, or output.
